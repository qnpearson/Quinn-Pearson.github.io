About Me

Hi, I’m Quinn Pearson, a New York–based data scientist and graduate student at the Georgia Institute of Technology, specializing in machine learning. I use data to understand complex systems, uncover patterns, and support decisions that matter.

My work has spanned start-ups to Fortune 500 environments, but I’m now focused on applying my technical skills toward public-minded goals. I’m especially interested in using data science for economic and policy analysis, evidence-based research, and building technology that strengthens social and humanitarian outcomes.

I’m excited to collaborate with organizations that share that mission — anywhere rigorous analysis and thoughtful engineering can create real impact.

If you’d like to connect or explore opportunities aligned with this kind of work, you can reach me by email or LinkedIn. I enjoy meeting others who care about mission-focused problem-solving.
